Same Apple.
Different offer in the EU and the US.

Apple sells the same iPhone on both sides of the Atlantic, but not the same experience. Some features never cross the ocean, some arrive late, some depend on which EU country you live in, and a few exist only in Europe. Pick your country to see what applies to you.

Sources: Apple's own support, product, developer and legal pages, plus the European Commission. Scope: the 27 EU member states versus the 50 US states. Not the UK, Norway, Iceland or Switzerland.

The big picture

It is not one-directional.

The US gets the newest AI, the richest subscription bundle and every satellite service. The EU gets app-store freedom, stronger consumer rights, a SIM tray, and blood-oxygen readings on the wrist. And a surprising amount depends on which member state, and which language, you use.

Inside the EU

Which EU? It depends on the country.

There is no single "European version" of Apple. Fitness+ is sold in 12 member states, satellite SOS works in 10, the top Apple One tier exists in 7, Vision Pro in 2. Pick a feature to light up the map, or click a country to make it yours.

Apple Intelligence & Siri

Late, then partial, then late again.

Apple Intelligence reached EU iPhones five months after the US. The new Siri AI, rolling out in the US since 14 September 2026, is explicitly withheld from EU iPhones, iPads and Watches. And whether any of it works in your language is a separate question.

What EU iPhones have

Writing Tools, Genmoji, Image Playground, Clean Up, notification and mail summaries, Visual Intelligence, ChatGPT integration, Live Translation in Messages, calls and AirPods. All since iOS 18.4 (31 March 2025) or later.

What EU iPhones lack

Siri AI (personal context, onscreen awareness, app actions, Write with Siri, Siri mode in Camera), iPhone Mirroring on Mac, and built-in call recording. Apple cites the Digital Markets Act; the Commission says the law does not prohibit launching them.

The language rule

Device language and Siri language must be the same supported language. Apple Intelligence supports 9 of the EU's 24 official languages. Speakers of the other 15 must run their iPhone in another language to use any of it.

Subscriptions & media

The bundle is bigger in America.

Apple One Individual and Family are the same four services everywhere. The difference is at the top: the US Premier tier adds Fitness+, News+ and 2 TB. Seven EU countries get a five-service Premium without News+. The rest get no top tier at all.

What is in the top Apple One tier, country by country

Highest tier advertised on each country's Apple One page. Prices are monthly, in local currency, tax included in the EU and before sales tax in the US. Eleven member states have no Apple One page on apple.com at all, although Apple's services register lists Apple One as available in all but Croatia and Romania.

Three things Europeans cannot buy at all

Apple News+

Sold only in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. No EU member state is on the list, and using English does not change that. In the US it is $12.99 a month for 500+ publications, narrated articles and puzzles.

Formula 1 on Apple TV

Every 2026 race, practice, qualifying and Sprint session is included for US Apple TV subscribers, together with F1 TV Premium. Those are US rights only. MLS, by contrast, is included everywhere since 2026.

No Fitness+ in 15 member states

Fitness+ is sold in 12 EU countries and absent from 15, including Denmark, Czechia, Greece and Hungary. Workouts are in English with subtitles in six languages and dubbing in German and Spanish for selected sessions.

One EU-only advantage. Under EU portability rules, your subscriptions travel with you: Apple's terms let you use your home-country services during a temporary stay in any other member state. This does not unlock services your home country lacks.

Apps & platform freedoms

The DMA dividend.

Since iOS 17.4 in March 2024, the Digital Markets Act has made the EU the one place where an iPhone can install apps from outside the App Store, run a browser that is not WebKit underneath, and delete Safari. It is also the reason Apple gives for withholding some features.

Fine print. Alternative distribution requires an EU Apple Account and physical presence in the EU. Apps you already installed keep working when you travel, with updates for up to 90 days outside the EU. Purchases made outside Apple's system are not covered by Ask to Buy or purchase sharing, though Screen Time still works.
Coming 1 October 2026. Apple's developer page describes new EU business terms taking effect on that date and broader installation access while travelling in autumn 2026. Those were not yet in force on 25 September 2026.

Hardware, buying & support

Same iPhone, different radios, different price tag.

The EU iPhone 18 Pro keeps a physical SIM tray. The US model is eSIM-only, spends the freed space on a bigger battery (two more hours of video playback on Apple's own figures) and adds mmWave 5G. Apple's EU prices include VAT, the US price does not include sales tax, and the exchange rate decides who really pays more.

Satellite: four services, four lists

Emergency SOS via satellite covers 10 EU countries and follows where you physically are. Messages via satellite, Roadside Assistance via satellite and Find My via satellite are US-side extras; no EU country has Messages or Roadside.

Wallet is where the US pulls ahead

Apple Pay and Tap to Pay work across all 27 member states. Apple Cash, Apple Card, the Savings account, driver's licences in Wallet and the passport-based Digital ID all require US residency or a US device region.

Vision Pro, AppleCare One, Apple Upgrade

Vision Pro is sold in France and Germany only. AppleCare One's multi-device plan exists in France and Germany; the Family plan and the Klarna-backed Apple Upgrade lease are US-only.

Consumer rights

Where the EU customer is better protected.

Two years, by law

EU purchases from professional sellers carry a minimum two-year legal guarantee against faulty goods; some countries go further. Apple's own limited warranty is one year in both regions. The legal guarantee is a claim against the seller, separate from AppleCare.

A SIM tray and a choice of engine

EU iPhones keep a nano-SIM slot alongside eSIM, at the cost of the two extra hours of battery the eSIM-only US model gets. EU browsers may use engines other than WebKit. EU users can delete Safari, Messages, Camera and the App Store, and pick more default apps.

Blood Oxygen on the wrist

A rare hardware feature that is better in Europe: Apple Watches bought in the US since 18 January 2024 route blood-oxygen analysis through the iPhone. EU-bought Watches measure and show it on the Watch itself.

Method & sources

How to read this.

Everything here is taken from Apple's own published pages, accessed on 25 September 2026, plus the European Commission for the legal position. It records what Apple publishes, not device tests.

"Unavailable" versus "not listed"

"Explicitly unavailable" means Apple states a restriction. "Not listed" means a country or language is missing from Apple's availability page. A localised marketing page alone does not establish eligibility, and a capability existing for developers does not mean every bank, browser or accessory has implemented it.

Eight variables that change the answer

Device language, Siri language, device region, Apple Account country, physical location, content language, hardware and OS version, and the provider (bank, carrier, transit operator). Two people in the same city can get different results.

Prices

Advertised standard prices in local currency. EU prices include VAT; US prices exclude sales tax, which varies by state. No currency conversion is asserted except in the adjustable price explorer, which uses the EUR/USD rate of 25 September 2026 as a default.

Known uncertainties

Apple's documents disagree on AirPods Live Translation in the EU. Vision Pro's country list comes from a 2025 newsroom footnote. Eleven EU countries have no Apple One page on apple.com, so their tiers are unknown. Apple's October 2026 EU app terms and extra Siri AI languages should be rechecked once in force.
