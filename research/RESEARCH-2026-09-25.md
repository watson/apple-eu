# Apple in the EU versus the US

**Research date: 25 September 2026**  
**Focus: EU availability, with Denmark examined separately**  
**Sources accessed: 25 September 2026**

Apple's US offering is broader in several subscriptions and mobile features. Denmark also lacks services sold in other EU countries. There is no single "European version" of Apple's offering: country, language, device, subscription tier and account eligibility can each change the answer.

## Main findings

| Question | Finding as of this report |
| --- | --- |
| Can English UI and Danish Siri work together with Apple Intelligence? | Apple's matching-language requirement still excludes that combination. [A1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115) |
| Is Apple Intelligence unavailable in the EU? | No. The newer Siri AI has a separate EU iPhone/iPad/Watch exclusion; EU Mac and Vision Pro access is available in English. [A2](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893) |
| Is Apple One the same price with fewer services? | The defensible comparison is country- and tier-specific. Denmark has no Premier; Germany/France's top tier omits News+. [Danish plans](https://www.apple.com/dk/apple-one/), [German plans](https://www.apple.com/de/apple-one/), [French plans](https://www.apple.com/fr/apple-one/) |
| Which subscription gaps matter most in Denmark? | News+ and Fitness+ are unavailable. US Apple TV includes F1 rights that its Danish counterpart lacks. [News+ eligibility](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102209), [Fitness+ availability](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/), [F1 rights](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/formula-1-begins-this-weekend-exclusively-on-apple-tv-in-the-us/) |
| Does the EU only lose features? | No. EU users gain alternative app distribution and broader default-app choices. [P03], [P04] |

## Scope and how to read the findings

This is a broad consumer-offering comparison, not an inventory of every Apple feature, bank, carrier, city or media title. It prioritizes differences that change what a customer can use or buy. The subscription table samples Denmark, Germany, France, the Netherlands and Sweden; it does not extrapolate those prices to all 27 EU countries. The UK, Norway, Iceland and Switzerland are outside the EU. Some rules cover the wider EEA instead.

Each claim follows the exact scope of its source. "Explicitly unavailable" records a direct Apple restriction. "Absent" or "not listed" means a country or language is missing from Apple's relevant availability list. A localized marketing or support page alone does not establish eligibility. A provider API being available does not prove that every bank, browser or accessory implements it.

These variables must be kept separate:

| Variable | Example of what it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Device/UI language | The language of system menus and some feature prerequisites. |
| Siri language | The primary assistant language. |
| Device region | Formatting and some feature conditions; it is not the same as residence. |
| Apple Account country/storefront | Which subscriptions, purchases or account-bound services are eligible. |
| Physical location | Satellite coverage and EU alternative-distribution eligibility. |
| Content or conversation language | Whether a particular text, recording or conversation is supported. |
| Hardware and OS version | Whether the model and software support the feature at all. |
| Provider or issuer | Whether a bank, transit operator, carrier or accessory enables a supported capability. |

The sections below supply the evidence for these distinctions. Prices are advertised standard prices unless marked as launch prices. This research did not change accounts, buy subscriptions or reproduce the findings on a configured iPhone. The reported English-UI/Danish-Siri failure is consistent with Apple's current rule, but its original OS version and failing feature were not independently established.

## Apple Intelligence, Siri and languages

### The English interface and Danish Siri issue remains

Danish is supported by Apple Intelligence, but the device and Siri languages must match. Apple's EU documentation permits cross-app capabilities including Photos editing, Image Playground and intelligent browsing. [A1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)

| Setup in Denmark, on compatible hardware | Published requirement outcome |
|---|---|
| English interface + Danish Siri | Does not satisfy Apple Intelligence's matching-language requirement. |
| English interface + English Siri | Satisfies the language requirement; individual feature restrictions still apply. |
| Danish interface + Danish Siri | Satisfies the language requirement; Danish is not supported by every feature. |

This is a requirements analysis, not a device test. Adding a secondary language is not a documented exception. [A1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)

The relevant settings are separate. General > Language & Region contains the device language and regional settings, such as calendar, measurement system, date and number formats. Siri > Language selects the language for Siri; Apple says this also affects Maps directions and Safari's Listen to Page. The settings guide separately allows additional languages for reading messages aloud. That is not documented as permission to use a different primary Siri language with Apple Intelligence. [A12](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-the-language-and-region-iphce20717a3/27/ios/27), [A13](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-siri-settings-iphc28624b81abc/27/ios/27)

Content language is another variable. Apple has explicitly documented Writing Tools and Smart Reply adapting to the supported language a person writes in. Therefore English device/Siri settings do not imply that every supported document must be English. That does not establish support for arbitrary mixed-language conversations or eliminate the matching-settings requirement. [A15](https://www.apple.com/in/newsroom/2025/03/apple-intelligence-features-are-now-available-in-india/)

### New Siri AI is a separate, current EU exclusion

The new Siri AI beta is currently English-only. It is unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, but available on macOS 27 and visionOS 27 with an EU Apple Account and both device and Siri languages set to English. Apple's setup flow includes a waitlist. Eligibility therefore does not mean immediate activation. Its capabilities include personal-context retrieval, onscreen awareness, broader app actions, a synced conversation-history app, and writing with Siri. [A2](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893)

For a Danish iPhone user, switching Siri to English does not remove this EU restriction. Danish Siri AI is unavailable in the US too. [A2](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893)

Apple began the Siri AI beta rollout on 2026-09-14. The launch announcement also excludes features dependent on Siri AI from EU iOS, iPadOS and watchOS. It announced French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish for the following month. As of this report, those are future availability commitments, not current supported languages. Danish is absent from that announced expansion. The new Siri mode in Camera and Siri AI-based writing or visual experiences must not be confused with the older features bearing similar names. [A3](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/)

EU users still have documented Writing Tools for proofreading, rewriting, summarizing and composing through ChatGPT in supported contexts. Apple's current European iPhone guide describes these directly. The Siri AI delay does not mean every generative writing tool has disappeared. [A5](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph6f08da1d2/ios)

### Danish support varies by individual feature

Apple's current availability table lists Danish for Writing Tools, Genmoji, Mail and Messages summaries, priority notifications, notification summaries, Photos search and memories, and older Visual Intelligence tasks. Danish is absent from Notes transcription summaries and voicemail summaries. Advanced Dictation and Mail Suggestions list English locales; Wallet's AI order tracking lists English UK/US. These language-locale labels should not be converted into unsupported claims about required physical countries. [A4](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/)

| Feature | Practical distinction for Denmark |
|---|---|
| Messages Live Translation | Danish is supported. Downloaded input/output languages and the conversation's translation settings are separate from the system language. Apple's Mac guide explicitly lists Danish and allows choosing the translated language within a conversation. [A6](https://support.apple.com/en-my/guide/mac-help/mchl58dfbdba/mac) |
| Phone and FaceTime Live Translation | Danish speech is not on the supported list. Apple's guide lists English UK/US, French France, German, Italian, Portuguese Brazil, Spanish Spain, Chinese simplified/traditional, Japanese and Korean for Phone. Availability of Danish text translation does not imply Danish call translation. [A7](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/iph22b72984d/ios) |
| Workout Buddy | The current watchOS 27 guide specifies English or Spanish for device and Siri language, an Apple Intelligence-enabled paired iPhone and Bluetooth headphones. Danish Apple Intelligence support does not provide a Danish workout coach. [A10](https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/watch/apd65c7938e6/watchos) |
| Notes audio transcription | Apple's current iPhone guide requires iPhone 12 or later and a supported device language. Its list is English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Japanese, Korean and simplified/traditional Chinese. Danish is absent. Apple separately warns that some languages supporting AI summaries do not support audio transcription. An English interface therefore does not make a Danish recording a supported transcription language. [A11](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iphbe11247b5/ios) |

### AirPods Live Translation needs a documentation caveat

The original EU delay should not be presented as a clean current restriction. Apple's 2025-11-04 announcement scheduled the EU expansion for December 2025. Its 2026-09-14 dedicated support article no longer states an EU exclusion and documents current compatible AirPods, an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone, the Translate app, current firmware and downloaded source/target languages. Together, these support treating the geographic delay as resolved, with a source inconsistency noted below. Danish speech remains absent from the current Apple Intelligence language list for this feature. [A9](https://www.apple.com/ie/newsroom/2025/11/live-translation-on-airpods-expands-to-the-eu/), [A8](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123185), [A4](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/)

Apple's current Irish AirPods Pro page also advertises Live Translation, providing current EU-market corroboration. [A16](https://www.apple.com/ie/airpods-pro/)

However, the undated UK iPhone translation guide still says AirPods Live Translation is unavailable in the EU. That conflicts with the specific EU expansion announcement and newer dedicated support article. This report gives the latter sources precedence but flags the disagreement; a real-device check would resolve the remaining documentation uncertainty for the reported setup. The current dedicated article lists AirPods 4 ANC, AirPods 5, AirPods Pro 2/3 and AirPods Max 2. It labels Live Translation a beta in its setup steps. [A7](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/iph22b72984d/ios), [A8](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123185)

### Historical delays and future charges

The first EU iPhone/iPad Apple Intelligence availability arrived on 2025-03-31 with iOS/iPadOS 18.4. Statements that Apple Intelligence as a whole is unavailable in the EU describe an earlier state. The 2026 Siri AI restriction is a different, narrower exclusion. [A14](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/03/apple-intelligence-features-expand-to-new-languages-and-regions-today/)

Some cloud features have daily limits. Apple plans paid expanded access, but this page establishes no current paid tier or regional price difference. [A1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)

## Subscriptions and media

### Apple One: fewer choices in Denmark, but compare matching tiers

Apple does not sell one uniform European Apple One package. The main Danish disadvantage is the absence of a Premier tier, Fitness+ and News+. Germany and France have a five-service Premium tier, while the US has a six-service Premier tier. These are different packages at different local prices. A claim that Europeans universally pay the same price for fewer services is too broad. The country and tier must be specified.

| Market | Individual, monthly | Family, monthly | Premier/Premium, monthly | Highest tier offered |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| United States | $21.95 | $27.95 | $39.95 | Six services, 2TB; includes Fitness+ and News+. [US Apple One](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/) |
| Denmark | 179 DKK | 239 DKK | Not offered | Four services, 200GB. [Danish Apple One](https://www.apple.com/dk/apple-one/) |
| Germany | €19.95 | €25.95 | €34.95 | Five services, 2TB; Fitness+, no News+. [German Apple One](https://www.apple.com/de/apple-one/) |
| France | €19.95 | €25.95 | €34.95 | Five services, 2TB; Fitness+, no News+. [French Apple One](https://www.apple.com/fr/apple-one/) |
| Netherlands | €19.95 | €25.95 | Not offered | Four services, 200GB. [Dutch Apple One](https://www.apple.com/nl/apple-one/) |
| Sweden | 215 SEK | 295 SEK | Not offered | Four services, 200GB. [Swedish Apple One](https://www.apple.com/se/apple-one/) |

Individual and Family in these markets contain the same four service categories: Music, TV, Arcade and iCloud+. Individual has 50GB and Family has 200GB. Family supports up to six people. News+ and Fitness+ are not included in US Individual or Family either. Their inclusion is a benefit of US Premier. German and French Premium replace Family's 200GB with 2TB and add Fitness+. Compare these tiers before interpreting a price difference. [US plans](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/), [German plans](https://www.apple.com/de/apple-one/), [French plans](https://www.apple.com/fr/apple-one/).

The Danish Family components separately cost 25 DKK for 200GB, 79 DKK for TV, 199 DKK for family Music and 59 DKK for Arcade. Their 362 DKK total exceeds Family by 123 DKK. This measures a discount for someone who wants all four services; it does not value Denmark's missing offerings. [Danish price breakdown](https://www.apple.com/dk/apple-one/).

For more storage, Denmark can add a separate 2TB iCloud+ plan at 89 DKK monthly. Family plus that plan costs 239 + 89 = 328 DKK monthly, with 200GB + 2TB = 2.2TB of storage. Apple documents keeping both allocations. [Storage with Apple One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108104). This adds storage but does not provide the missing subscriptions. The 2TB standalone prices are $9.99 in the US and €9.99 in Apple's euro pricing group. Apple explicitly marks Danish and euro iCloud prices as tax-inclusive, while some US states add tax. Exchange rates, taxes, differing storage and personal demand must be normalized before claiming equal prices or a percentage value loss. No currency conversion or universal value percentage is asserted here. [iCloud+ pricing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047).

### News+ and Fitness+ have separate country and language restrictions

News+ is available only in Australia, Canada, the UK and the US. No EU member state is on Apple's list. The UK is European but is not in the EU. Consequently, using English in Denmark does not itself make a Danish News+ subscription available. Apple also warns that capabilities differ among the four supported markets. [Subscribe to Apple News+](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102209), published 2025-10-23.

The US subscription costs $12.99 monthly and includes more than 500 participating publishers, narrated articles and puzzles, with sharing for up to six people. Its omission is a meaningful potential loss for a reader who would use it. It is not automatically a $12.99 loss for every European customer, because readership and desired publications differ. [US News+](https://www.apple.com/apple-news/).

Fitness+ remains unavailable in Denmark. Apple's current list includes these 12 EU members: Austria, Belgium, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain and Sweden. The US is also supported. The US standalone subscription is $9.99 monthly or $79.99 annually. This is a country rollout difference within the EU, not an EU-wide prohibition. [Fitness+ availability and pricing](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/).

The Netherlands and Sweden joined the service on 2025-12-15, yet their current Apple One pages still offer only Individual and Family. Availability of Fitness+ as a separate subscription therefore does not establish availability of a higher Apple One bundle. Apple's December expansion also added Belgium, Finland and Poland. [Fitness+ expansion](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/12/apple-fitness-plus-expands-to-28-new-markets/), published 2025-12-08; [Dutch Apple One](https://www.apple.com/nl/apple-one/), [Swedish Apple One](https://www.apple.com/se/apple-one/).

Fitness+ has another restriction after country eligibility: workout language. Apple describes original English instruction, subtitles in English, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian and Spanish, and selected workouts dubbed with generated trainer voices in German, Spanish and Japanese. Danish is not listed for audio or subtitles. Dubbing does not imply every episode is dubbed. Apple's announcement specifies selection of an available audio track inside a workout, so language support is separate from the country where the subscription is sold. [Fitness+ language announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/12/apple-fitness-plus-expands-to-28-new-markets/), published 2025-12-08.

### Apple TV: sports rights produce a clear content difference

For 2026, US Apple TV subscribers receive all Formula 1 races, practice, qualifying and Sprint sessions. F1 TV Premium is included without another subscription charge. Apple's footnote requires a US Apple TV subscription. Denmark and other EU countries do not receive those US rights merely by buying Apple TV, changing the interface language or owning an Apple TV box. This gives a US subscriber who watches F1 additional value within a service category that both markets have. Apple's source also describes English and Spanish commentary. [Apple's F1 launch](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/formula-1-begins-this-weekend-exclusively-on-apple-tv-in-the-us/), published 2026-03-05.

MLS is different. Since the 2026 season, all matches are included in the normal Apple TV subscription in more than 100 markets, including Denmark; the old extra MLS Season Pass should not be treated as the current package. The Danish live MLS page explicitly offers the 2026 season with Apple TV at 79 DKK monthly. Danish subscribers receive this benefit too. [Danish MLS announcement](https://www.apple.com/dk/newsroom/2025/11/major-league-soccer-is-coming-to-apple-tv-starting-in-2026/), published 2025-11-13; [current Danish MLS subscription](https://tv.apple.com/dk/channel/mls/tvs.sbd.7000?l=da).

Friday Night Baseball is distributed to 60 countries and regions. Do not equate its international availability with every part of Apple's US baseball offering: Apple's 2026 season announcement specifically describes MLB Big Inning and related daily programming for US fans. Sports availability must be checked by competition and programme. A general Apple TV subscription is not proof of an identical sports catalogue. [2026 baseball announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/friday-night-baseball-returns-to-apple-tv-on-march-27-for-its-fifth-season/), published 2026-03-11.

### Music, Arcade, iCloud and other media

Apple's current media availability register lists Music, Music Classical, Arcade, TV subscriptions, Podcasts and paid Books/audiobooks in both Denmark and the US. It also exposes a smaller purchasing difference: US customers can buy movies and TV shows; Denmark lists movie purchases but not TV-show purchases. A service's presence does not prove an identical catalogue: Apple says media availability and capabilities can vary by country. No catalogue-size comparison was established in this research. [Media services availability](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205), published 2026-09-15.

iCloud+ offers the same published storage tier sizes from 50GB through 12TB in Denmark and the US. Denmark's 200GB Apple One ceiling is a bundle limitation, not a maximum iCloud storage limit. [iCloud+ plans](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047), published 2026-09-16.

Apple's Danish media terms also give EU residents an advantage relevant to travel: services can be used in the home country and any other EU member state during a temporary stay. A trip within the EU is therefore different from permanently changing the account's home country. This portability provision does not create access to a subscription that was unavailable in the home country. [Danish media terms, section A](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dk/terms.html), updated 2026-09-14.

### Creator Studio is a separate bundle

Apple Creator Studio is available in Denmark and the US according to the media register. It is a separate creative-app subscription and does not appear among the subscriptions included in either country's Apple One plans. That omission applies to both markets. [Media availability](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205), [US Apple One](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/), [Danish Apple One](https://www.apple.com/dk/apple-one/).

Its current US product page lists $12.99 monthly or $129 annually. It includes Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, Pixelmator Pro, Motion, Compressor and MainStage, plus premium content and capabilities in Keynote, Pages, Numbers and Freeform. Standard subscriptions can be shared; education subscriptions cannot. The ordinary productivity apps remain usable free. [Creator Studio](https://www.apple.com/apple-creator-studio/).

Apple's Danish launch announcement quoted 99 DKK monthly or 990 DKK annually, and 19 DKK monthly or 190 DKK annually for eligible higher-education users. These are the published Danish launch prices, not a checkout verification on the research date. [Danish Creator Studio announcement](https://www.apple.com/dk/newsroom/2026/01/introducing-apple-creator-studio-an-inspiring-collection-of-creative-apps/), published 2026-01-13. The available evidence does not establish a Denmark-wide reduced app bundle. Individual AI capabilities still have hardware, regional and usage restrictions; purchasing the bundle is not proof that every advertised intelligent feature will work on every configuration. [Creator Studio requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125029), published 2026-06-30.

Apple's product restriction is not a statement that every form of call recording is illegal throughout the EU. [P02] |

EU alternative distribution also changes who handles purchases. Apple does not provide its usual purchase sharing, Ask to Buy, or in-app-purchase restrictions for purchases made outside its purchase system. Ordinary Screen Time and parental controls still work. Apple's current consumer support page says existing alternative apps remain usable when travelling, with updates for up to 90 days outside the eligible region; new installations require being in that region. [P03]

There is a pending change worth keeping separate. Apple's developer page describes broader travel installation access in fall 2026 and new business terms effective **1 October 2026**, after this report's date. The current consumer support instructions above are the basis for the present-tense travel finding. The announced fee and eligibility changes should not be described as already effective on 25 September. [P10]

External payment links are not uniquely European. Apple's current App Review Guidelines exempt US storefront apps from the general prohibition on links and calls to action directing users to other purchase methods. The EU has its own alternative-payment rules. A comparison that says only EU apps can link to web purchases is out of date. [P11]

### What can be attributed to the DMA

Apple attributes the EU Siri AI delay to disagreements over DMA interoperability and says it has no timetable for the EU iPhone/iPad release. The European Commission disputes that interpretation: it says the DMA does not prohibit new products and that Apple must provide compliant third-party access. These are the two parties' positions, not an independent finding that EU law bans Siri AI. The observed availability restriction is separate from the disputed explanation. [P12], [P13]

The same caution applies to iPhone Mirroring. Apple confirms the EU restriction; the Commission says the law does not prohibit launching it. There is no basis here to attribute Apple News+, Danish language omissions, sports rights, or every other country difference to the DMA. [P01], [P13]

Home Screen web apps should not be listed as a current EU loss. Apple reversed its proposed removal in March 2024, as the Commission's account records. [P13]

### Watch, hearing and Maps

Apple lists Denmark and the US for ECG, AFib History, sleep-apnoea notifications and hypertension notifications. Country support still requires compatible hardware and the feature's individual eligibility rules. [S13](https://www.apple.com/watchos/feature-availability/)

AirPods hearing functionality needs a model-level comparison. Hearing Test and Hearing Aid are available in Denmark and the US on supported AirPods Pro 2 and Pro 3. But the separately named Hearing Protection feature on Pro 2 is listed for the US and selected other jurisdictions, with no EU member country listed. Hearing Protection on Pro 3 includes Denmark and numerous EU countries. A statement that "AirPods hearing health is unavailable in Europe" would therefore be wrong; a statement that Pro 2 and Pro 3 have identical Danish hearing features would also be wrong. [S14](https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/feature-availability/)

Blood Oxygen is a difference in the EU's favour for some hardware. For specified US-purchased Watch models bought on or after 2024-01-18, identified by part-number endings LW/A or LS/A, Apple now performs analysis on the iPhone and displays results in its Health app. The older blanket statement that recent US Watches cannot measure blood oxygen is outdated. This is tied to the purchased hardware variant; it is not a Danish-language limitation. [S15](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120358)

Apple Watch For Your Kids is listed for the US and several EU markets, including France, Germany, Poland, Spain and Sweden; Denmark is absent. Wallet transit support likewise lists US cities and selected EU systems, including Paris, Skåne and Turku, but not Denmark. [S13](https://www.apple.com/watchos/feature-availability/) Transit payment support must be distinguished from an agency's own ticketing app or accepting ordinary contactless payment cards. Apple directs users to the local operator for current card acceptance. [S16](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118625)

Maps in Denmark supports cycling, transit, Look Around and custom routes; Copenhagen also has Detailed City Experience. Visited Places lists the US but not Denmark or EU member countries by name, although Réunion is separately listed. [A4](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/)

Watch Maps' hikes and topographic maps list the US and eight EU countries: Austria, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Portugal, Slovenia and Spain. Denmark is absent. [S13](https://www.apple.com/watchos/feature-availability/)

### Hardware, AppleCare and purchasing

Clean Energy Charging remains US-only in Apple's current iOS 27 guide. It uses local grid forecasts to schedule charging for periods with cleaner electricity. Denmark does not receive that integration. Apple's related EnergyKit framework, which can inform EV charging and thermostat apps, is limited to the contiguous United States. These are distinct from ordinary battery charging optimisation. [S29](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/charge-with-cleaner-energy-sources-iphc49d61e92/27/ios/27), [S30](https://developer.apple.com/energykit/)

The same iPhone product name can cover different physical configurations. The current Danish iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max specifications list nano-SIM plus eSIM, dual-eSIM support and sub-6 GHz 5G. US specifications list eSIM-only operation and mmWave as well as sub-6 GHz. Thus the Danish variant retains a physical SIM option, while the US variant supports an additional 5G radio technology. This is a comparison of the named models, not a claim about every current or historical iPhone. [S18](https://www.apple.com/dk/iphone-18-pro/specs/), [S19](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/)

Apple Vision Pro is available in the US and selected countries. Apple's August 2026 country list names France and Germany within the EU, not Denmark. Importing it does not remove account and support limitations. Apple's live purchase FAQ requires a supported-country Apple Account for its App Store and Apple Music/TV purchases, and says support is available only in countries where Vision Pro is sold. ZEISS insert prescriptions and delivery also have purchase-country restrictions. The availability of a Danish Apple Newsroom article about Vision Pro should not be read as Danish retail availability. ## Warranty and consumer protection

An offering comparison should account for consumer rights as well as subscription extras. EU purchases from professional sellers carry a minimum two-year legal guarantee for faulty goods, with some national rules providing longer protection. This is a claim against the seller and is distinct from optional AppleCare. [P14]

Apple's Danish warranty document explicitly distinguishes its one-year voluntary manufacturer warranty from the Danish two-year right to complain about defects to the seller. Apple's US hardware warranty also specifies one year, subject to applicable consumer rights. Consequently, neither "Europe only gets Apple's one-year warranty" nor "Apple gives every European product a two-year manufacturer warranty" is an accurate general comparison. This report does not attempt a US state-by-state consumer-law comparison. [P15], [P16]

## Remaining uncertainties and refresh priorities

- AirPods translation. current dedicated support and EU product pages support availability, but an iPhone guide retains an EU exclusion. The conflicting sources are identified above.
- Account and travel edge cases. Apple's Siri AI documentation does not spell out every combination of residence, current location and account country. No account-switching workaround is established here.
- Catalogue and value. this report verifies service categories and selected sports rights, not title-by-title Music/TV/Books parity. The value of News+, Fitness+ or F1 depends on whether the subscriber would use them. No universal percentage loss of value is claimed.
- Prices and rollout dates. Creator Studio's Danish price is explicitly a published launch price. Apple lists October 2026 changes for EU app terms and additional Siri AI languages; those should be rechecked after they take effect.
- Device-level behaviour. language lists, hardware requirements and the actual OS version should be checked together before interpreting a missing setting. The report records documented support, not comparative quality measurements.

For future updates, retain this dated snapshot and create a new dated report. Recheck the dedicated support article and local product page for each changed finding, record the page's own date separately from the access date, and distinguish an announcement from a released feature.
